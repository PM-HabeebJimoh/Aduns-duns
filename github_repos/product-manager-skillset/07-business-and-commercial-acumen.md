# 7. Business & Commercial Acumen

Product decisions are business decisions. PMs must understand **how the company makes money** and how their product contributes.

## Sub-skills

- **Unit economics** — CAC, LTV, LTV/CAC ratio, payback period, gross margin, contribution margin.
- **SaaS metrics** — MRR/ARR, churn (logo vs. revenue), NRR, GRR, expansion, quick ratio, Rule of 40.
- **Marketplace/consumer metrics** — take rate, GMV, DAU/MAU, K-factor, viral coefficient.
- **Pricing & packaging** — value-based vs. cost-plus, tiering, seat vs. usage, discounting discipline.
- **Go-to-market (GTM)** — PLG vs. sales-led vs. hybrid, launch tiers, positioning, messaging.
- **Sales fluency** — pipeline stages, deal cycles, MEDDIC/BANT, working with AEs and SEs.
- **Customer success & support** — churn signals, health scores, escalation flows.
- **Competitive & market analysis** — teardowns, win/loss analysis, analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester).
- **P&L literacy** — reading a basic income statement, understanding where your product sits.
- **Regulatory awareness** (industry-dependent) — HIPAA, PCI, SOC 2, GDPR, financial regs.

## Why it matters

Features that don't move business outcomes get killed. PMs fluent in the numbers get bigger budgets, teams, and scope.

## What great looks like

- Can explain how a proposed feature affects CAC, LTV, or retention.
- Talks to sales/CS as often as to engineers.
- Doesn't over-index on revenue at the expense of long-term product health.
- Builds business cases execs actually approve.

## Common pitfalls

- Ignoring the sales team's field intel.
- Building for revenue *this quarter* at the cost of retention.
- Confusing bookings with revenue with cash.
- Copying competitors' pricing without understanding their cost structure.
