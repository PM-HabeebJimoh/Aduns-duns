# PM Skills Self-Assessment

Rate yourself on each sub-skill using this scale:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Unaware — I don't know what this is |
| 2 | Aware — I understand the concept but haven't applied it |
| 3 | Practicing — I use this with guidance / inconsistently |
| 4 | Proficient — I use this independently and well |
| 5 | Coaching — I can teach others and set the bar for the team |

## How to use

1. Copy this file into a private note.
2. Score every sub-skill from the eight skill-area files.
3. Circle your **three lowest** scores that matter most for your current role/level.
4. Build a 90-day learning plan against those three, using the resources at the bottom of each skill file.
5. Re-score every 6 months.

## Recommended target scores by level

| Skill Area | APM | PM | Senior PM | Group / Principal |
|------------|-----|----|-----------|-------------------|
| Product Discovery | 2–3 | 3–4 | 4 | 4–5 |
| Product Strategy | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Execution & Delivery | 3–4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Analytics & Data | 3 | 3–4 | 4 | 4 |
| Leadership & Communication | 3 | 3–4 | 4 | 5 |
| Technical Fluency | 2–3 | 3 | 3–4 | 4 |
| Business & Commercial Acumen | 2 | 3 | 4 | 4–5 |
| Design & UX Sense | 2–3 | 3 | 3–4 | 4 |

> These are heuristics, not rules. A PM at a dev-tools company will lean higher on Technical Fluency; a PM at a fintech will lean higher on Business Acumen and Regulatory awareness.
