# 5. Leadership & Communication

PMs have **responsibility without authority**. Everything gets done through influence, clarity, and trust.

## Sub-skills

- **Stakeholder management** — mapping stakeholders, understanding their goals, managing up/down/sideways.
- **Influence without authority** — building coalitions, trading favors, framing asks in stakeholder terms.
- **Written communication** — crisp docs, exec summaries, weekly updates, async-first writing.
- **Verbal & presentation skills** — running meetings, executive reviews, product demos, all-hands narratives.
- **Storytelling** — turning data + user insight into a narrative that motivates action.
- **Conflict resolution** — resolving eng-vs-design or PM-vs-sales disagreements productively.
- **Negotiation** — scope, timelines, resources, priorities.
- **Feedback culture** — giving and receiving feedback; running blameless post-mortems.
- **Team building & coaching** (senior PMs) — hiring, mentoring, growing other PMs.
- **Emotional intelligence** — self-awareness, empathy, reading the room.

## Why it matters

Great ideas die from bad communication. A mediocre idea communicated brilliantly often beats a brilliant idea communicated poorly.

## What great looks like

- Every stakeholder knows what's happening and why, without being chased.
- Meetings end with clear decisions, owners, and next steps.
- Difficult conversations happen early and directly, not passively.
- The team feels informed, unblocked, and motivated.

## Common pitfalls

- Being the messenger instead of the decision-driver.
- Avoiding conflict → building silent resentment.
- Over-communicating to look busy; under-communicating the hard stuff.
- Using jargon that excludes non-PM audiences.
